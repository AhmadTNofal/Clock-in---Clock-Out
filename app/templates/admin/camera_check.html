How to read this

  • Face pixels — the detected face width. Set FACE_MIN_PIXELS well below what a person standing at the kiosk measures; if it reads under about 80 the camera is too far away or too low-resolution.
  • Sharpness — focus measure. Set FACE_MIN_SHARPNESS to roughly half of a good reading, so ordinary movement is not rejected.
  • Motion — frame-to-frame change in the face. A live person should comfortably exceed LIVENESS_MIN_MOTION; a photo held up reads close to zero.
  • Consistency — how alike the frames are. Should be above about 0.8 for one person standing still.
